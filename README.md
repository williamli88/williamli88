**[Chorus](https://github.com/williamli88/chorus)** is a chore app for houses that keep score. It deals out chores on a rotation, asks for a photo as proof, keeps a credit ledger, lets roommates trade chores, and spins a month-end wheel of dares for whoever finishes last. It's in daily use in my own house. Its fall update added live presence, proofs that drop onto your board as they're filed, real weather, and a month-end recap you can share.

### Featured: Chang Jiang

<a href="https://github.com/williamli88/changjiang">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/williamli88/changjiang/main/assets/banner.jpg" alt="Chang Jiang" width="100%">
</a>

**[Chang Jiang](https://github.com/williamli88/changjiang)** is online ordering for a family Chinese kitchen in Madison. Customers order pickup or delivery and pay by card, couriers are dispatched automatically, and the kitchen cooks from a live board that rings when an order lands. Staff run the menu, stock, coupons and refunds themselves.
